Inspect plan/job receipts, current source state, artifact hashes and execution authority. Separate synthetic/protocol evidence from live kernel, tenant and engineer evidence.

Use `fusion_handoff_prepare` with explicit plan references. Include requirements/design intent, exact version/configuration, before/after facts, numerical checks with units, health findings, artifact manifests and manufacturing assets where relevant.

Keep incomplete checks and partial/unknown outcomes visible. A screenshot, generated toolpath, setup sheet, provider success or model assertion does not supply machine-collision or regulatory proof.

The output is a local draft. Do not send it, publish it, update other enterprise systems or advance a release without explicit destination authority and a qualified adapter. Existing draft scope should not cause repetitive permission requests.

Return the artifact and the remaining decisions with their practical impact. See [operations and recovery](../../docs/operations.md).
